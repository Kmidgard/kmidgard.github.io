About Me

Hello!

My name is Katie Midgarden. I’m an artist, photographer, and graphic design student at Arizona State University, who enjoys exploring creativity through drawing, painting, and digital design. I’m passionate about creating art that evokes emotion and tells a story. I love experimenting with different mediums and techniques to bring my ideas to life.
I started drawing and painting at a young age, and it has been a lifelong passion ever since. I enjoy capturing the beauty of the little things in life, from the colors of a sunset to the intricate details of a flower. I also enjoy exploring the world through photography, capturing moments and memories that can be cherished forever. I have enjoyed taking art classes throughout my life, and I am always looking for new ways to improve my skills and learn new techniques.
I had the opportunity to study visual arts in high school, where I learned the fundamentals of drawing, painting, and design. I also had the chance to further develop my skills through various art competitions and exhibitions, which helped me gain confidence in my abilities. I attended the University of North Dakota, where I obtained a BFA in visual arts. I am currently pursuing a degree in graphic design at Arizona State University, where I am excelling in my studies and continuing to develop my skills as an artist and designer.

I’m inspired by places, animals, nature, and experiences. This portfolio features a selection of my artwork and photography.
